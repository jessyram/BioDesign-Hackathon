Grip Health Insights

Why grip strength matters

Grip strength is a key indicator of hand function and overall muscle health. A decline may signal fatigue, injury, or nerve issues.

Common causes of decline

  • Repetitive strain
  • Carpal Tunnel Syndrome
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Muscle fatigue

Prevention tips

  • Take breaks while writing
  • Use ergonomic tools
  • Do grip-strength exercises
