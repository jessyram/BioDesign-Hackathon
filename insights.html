Grip Health Insights

What is grip strength?

Grip strength measures how strong your hand muscles are and is a key indicator of overall hand function.

Common causes of weak grip

  • Repetitive strain injuries
  • Carpal Tunnel Syndrome
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Muscle fatigue

Prevention tips

  • Take breaks when writing
  • Use ergonomic tools
  • Do hand strengthening exercises
